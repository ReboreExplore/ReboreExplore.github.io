I had the opportunity to give a talk on Datalad and Dataverse for the Computational Cognitive Science (SCCS) group retreat. The talk focused on the Datalad and Dataverse tools, which are essential for data management and versioning in neuroscience research. I discussed how these tools can be used in a standardized workflow for organizing, standardizing, and archiving data collected in large scale experiments, particularly in the context of EEG and Eye Tracking data. It was a cool opportunity of public speaking and sharing knowledge with my peers in the field.
