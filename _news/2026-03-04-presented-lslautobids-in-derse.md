I had the opportunity to do a oral presentation of my work on LSLAutoBIDS at the DERSE Conference 2026. The work was developed in the Computational Cognitive Science group at the University of Stuttgart and is a part of my research assistantship under the supervision of Prof. Benedikt Ehinger. LSLAutoBIDS is a data organization, standardization and archiving workflow for the EEG and Eye Tracking data collected in neuroscience experiments. The presentation was well received and I had some interesting discussions with other researchers in the field about the potential applications and future directions of this work.
